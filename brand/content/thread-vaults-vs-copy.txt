Why vaults beat copy-trading.

Copy-trading platforms let you follow individual trades. You copy each buy, each sell.

But the trader can de-authorize you mid-position. You're stuck holding while they exit.

Vaults solve this.

With vaults, you own shares of the pool.

The trader trades the entire pool. Your share value moves with their performance.

You can sell your shares anytime. The trader cannot stop you.

If the trader blows up, you lose whatever the pool lost — but you're never trapped.

Performance is measured per-share, transparent and verifiable on-chain.

70% of profits go to depositors (you). 30% goes to the trader.

No hidden fees. No withdrawal limits. No de-auth risk.

Back the best traders. They can never run.
