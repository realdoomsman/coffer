## The line we do not cross

The creator fee is earned by the **deployer wallet** and funds **vaults**.
It is not a payment to token holders. Never write it as one.

Do not post, in any wording:

- "holders earn", "revenue share", "dividends", "passive income", "APY"
- "buy before X", price targets, "early", "don't miss", "guaranteed"
- anything implying the token entitles you to vault profits or platform revenue

Those turn a memecoin into something a regulator reads as a security, and
they are also the sentences people quote back at you when it goes badly.
What is true is enough: fees go to a published wallet, that wallet funds
vaults, and every movement is on-chain.
