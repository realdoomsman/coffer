Why traders can't rug a Coffer vault.

Other copy-trade platforms: trader controls the wallet → they can rug.

Coffer: the vault program owns custody. Traders get scoped execute_swap instructions ONLY.

The execute_swap instruction is scoped:
- Can only call approved Jupiter routes
- Can only move tokens within the vault
- Cannot send funds to external addresses

The trader's private key never signs a transfer. Only the program does.
